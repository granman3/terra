# Presentation frameworks researched (2026-10-27)

For a short business pitch that goes problem → solution → how we get there.

| # | Framework | From | Steps | Best for | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS) | Copywriting tradition (often credited to Dan Kennedy) | Problem → make it hurt → solve | Short persuasive openers | https://copyblogger.com/problem-agitate-solve/ |
| 2 | Minto Pyramid / SCQA | Barbara Minto, McKinsey | Situation → Complication → Question → Answer | Executive briefs | https://thinkinsights.net/strategy/scqa-logic |
| 3 | Situation-Complication-Resolution | McKinsey practice | Situation → complication → resolution | One-slide summaries | https://managementconsulted.com/scqa-framework/ |
| 4 | Sequoia business-plan deck | Sequoia Capital | Purpose → Problem → Solution → Why now → Market → Competition → Product → Model → Team → Financials | Investor decks | https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/sequoia-capital-business-plan-template/ |
| 5 | YC Seed Deck | Aaron Harris, Y Combinator | One-liner → Problem → Solution → Traction → Market → Model → Team → Ask | Seed pitches | https://ycombinator.com/library/2u-how-to-build-your-seed-round-pitch-deck |
| 6 | 10/20/30 Rule | Guy Kawasaki | 10 slides, 20 minutes, 30-pt font: problem, solution, model, tech, go-to-market, competition, team, milestones | Keeping slides lean | https://www.cbsnews.com/news/improve-your-powerpoint-presentations-with-guy-kawasakis-10-20-30-rule/ |
| 7 | Sparkline ("what is / what could be") | Nancy Duarte, *Resonate* | What is ↔ what could be → call to action → new bliss | Vision talks | https://www.duarte.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-contrast/ |
| 8 | Story Spine | Kenn Adams (taught at Pixar) | Once upon a time → every day → one day → because of that → until finally → ever since | Story arc | https://www.npr.org/transcripts/nx-s1-5750619 |
| 9 | Monroe's Motivated Sequence | Alan Monroe, Purdue (1930s) | Attention → Need → Satisfaction → Visualization → Action | Short persuasive talks with an ask | https://www.hawaii.edu/mauispeech/pdf/mspguide.pdf |
| 10 | StoryBrand SB7 | Donald Miller | Character → problem → guide → plan → call to action → failure avoided → success | Customer messaging | https://grahammann.net/book-notes/building-a-storybrand-donald-miller |
| 11 | Heilmeier Catechism | George Heilmeier, DARPA | What are you doing? → How is it done today? → What's new? → Who cares? → Risks → Cost → How do you measure success? | Technical judges | https://murray.cds.caltech.edu/Heilmeier_Catechism |
| 12 | 3-minute hackathon pitch | Organizer guides (Reskilll, JetBrains) | Hook/problem → one-line solution → live demo → impact → ask | Hackathon judging | https://blog.jetbrains.com/ai/2026/06/how-to-win-a-hackathon-notes-from-the-judging-table/ |

## What the deck uses

**Monroe's Motivated Sequence**, checked against the **Heilmeier** questions a technical judge asks.

| Monroe step | Slide |
|---|---|
| Attention | 1 · Terra: one line + QR · 2 · 33 million data points, every government source |
| Need | 3 · The problem: Utah builds plants for its worst hour, and AI is making it worse ($ and Utah's 153 hours) |
| Satisfaction | 4 · Power plants can't move, AI work can · 5 · We don't spend energy to save energy (690,000×) |
| Visualization | 6 · Live map: what Utah saved this week (1.2 s per replay hour) |
| Action | 7 · Why it matters for Torus: moving the load + the day-ahead forecast, how we get there |
