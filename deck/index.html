The math inside Terra
One equation picks where and when AI work runs.
(r*, s*) = argmin over regions r, start s ≤ D−4 of Σ_h [ p̂(r,h) + g(r,h)/4000 ]
Pick the region r and start hour s (now, +3, +6 or +12 h, within the job's deadline D) where the job's 4 hours carry the least chance of rush hour, carbon as the tiebreak.
The forecast
p̂ = σ(f_θ(x) / T)
492 inputs per region per hour in, chance of rush hour for each of the next 24 h out. 561K learned parameters θ, calibrated by T.
How it learned
min BCE + 4·MSE(share) + 4·MSE(CO₂)
Rush hour, fuel share and carbon learned together, on 755K examples from 2022–25.
How it's graded
Brier = (1/N) Σ (p̂ − y)²
On 2026 hours it never saw: 0.050 vs 0.071 for the utility's own forecast.
Result: a 4-hour AI job's rush-hour exposure drops from 1.14 hours to 0.04.
